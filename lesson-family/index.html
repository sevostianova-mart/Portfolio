🏡 My Family

English · Grade 2
★ 0

Hello! 👋

Today we talk about my family. You will learn new words, play games and collect stars ⭐.

Goal: I can say who is who in my family.
Цель: я могу рассказать о членах своей семьи.

1. Warm-up

Who is who?

Look at the family. Tap the picture and answer.

2. Revision

Match the English word and the picture

Tap a word, then its pair. / Нажми слово, затем пару.

3. New words

Tap a card · listen 🔊 · repeat

4. Grammar

He is… / She is… / This is my…
This is my mother.   He is my father.   She is my sister.
👦 He = мальчик, мужчина   👧 She = девочка, женщина

5. Practice

Build the sentence

6. Feedback

How was the lesson?

I can say: This is my … ✓   I know 6 family words ✓   I can use he / she ✓

7. Homework 🏠

Домашнее задание
  • Draw your family (or a photo) and sign each person: This is my…
  • Learn 6 words: mother, father, sister, brother, grandma, grandpa.
  • Tell 3 sentences about your family to mum or dad.
